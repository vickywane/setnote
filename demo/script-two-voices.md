# Setnote demo script

**VOICEOVER:** Existing ElevenLabs Sia takes throughout, including the opening, readings and end card. Local manual takes have been removed. No new ElevenLabs credits were used for this revision.

The cut opens with one short, diverse gym scene of a lifter coming off a set, then moves straight into the product. It shows set logging, note parsing, the camera consent flow, a real SmartSpectra scan on an iPhone, the saved readings, a short architecture card and the privacy details. A low music bed continues through the cut and ducks under narration.

| Beat | Voice | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | ElevenLabs Sia | Last set. That one felt brutal. |
| Log sets | Narrator | Two taps. Your last set is ready to go, and your rest timer starts the moment you log. |
| Write it down | Narrator | Then scribble. Setnote reads the note and turns weight, sets and reps into a log with one tap. |
| Consent | Narrator | Setnote explains the check, and the camera waits until you tap Start. Every time. |
| SmartSpectra | Narrator | Presage SmartSpectra turns the front camera into a vitals sensor. It reads pulse and breathing live, on the phone. No strap. No watch. Nothing to charge. |
| Results | ElevenLabs Sia | Pulse seventy-seven. Breathing twenty-three. Saved right here, beside the set. |
| Architecture | Narrator | SmartSpectra runs on-device through a native Expo module. Only readings sync to Setnote in Supabase. |
| Privacy | ElevenLabs Sia | By default, your check video is not sent off the phone or saved. If you opt in, videos are private to your account; as a guest, they stay on your phone. Presage processes video on-device; only readings sync. |
| Outro | ElevenLabs Sia | Lift heavy. Listen to your body. Setnote. |
